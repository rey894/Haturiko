When you use our app or interact with our chatbot, we may collect:

How We Use Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and provide requested services.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information. Data may be shared only with:

Data Retention

We retain your chat data only as long as necessary to provide the service. Messages and logs may be stored temporarily but are periodically deleted.

Security
