Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 27, 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how Haturiko Services Inc. (“Haturiko”, “we”, “our”, “us”) collects, uses, and protects information when you interact with our Facebook/Messenger integrations, chatbots, and related webhooks (the “Services”).

Information We Collect

  • Account/Profile Data: Public profile info permitted by Facebook (e.g., name, profile photo, user ID).
  • Message Content: Text and payloads you send to the bot (including postbacks and quick replies) to operate the Services.
  • Technical Logs: Timestamps, event types, delivery/read receipts, and basic device or browser metadata sent by Facebook.

How We Use Information

  • Provide and improve the chatbot experience and respond to your requests.
  • Operate webhooks and automations (e.g., via n8n) necessary for the Services.
  • Maintain security, prevent abuse, and comply with applicable laws and Facebook Platform Policies.

Legal Bases

We process information based on your consent, our legitimate interests in operating and improving the Services, and/or to comply with legal obligations.

Sharing of Information

  • Service Providers: We may share limited data with vendors who host, process, or support our infrastructure (e.g., automation and hosting providers) under appropriate safeguards.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information if required by law, regulation, or valid legal request.
  • We do not sell or rent your personal information.

Data Retention

We retain chat events and logs only as long as necessary to provide the Services and for legitimate business purposes (e.g., debugging, security), after which we delete or de-identify them.

Security

We employ reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your Choices & Rights

  • You may stop interacting with the bot at any time or use Facebook controls to manage permissions.
  • You may request access, correction, or deletion of your information by contacting us at the email below. We will honor requests as required by applicable law.

Children’s Privacy

The Services are not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect such data.

International Transfers

Your information may be processed in countries other than your own. Where applicable, we use safeguards to protect personal data when transferred internationally.

Third-Party Services

Your use of Facebook and Messenger is subject to their own terms and privacy policies. We do not control Facebook’s processing of your data.

Changes to This Policy
